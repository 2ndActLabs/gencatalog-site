GenCatalog 5.16.88

Published August 3, 2026

Keep the Original. Keep the Story.

Every image-to-image edit and image-to-video generation begins somewhere. GenCatalog now does more to keep that starting image with the work it inspired—not just a link that may disappear later.

When Grok makes the original available, GenCatalog can preserve an exact local copy beside your catalog. Your creative lineage remains useful even if an account-protected URL eventually stops loading.

Bring Older Grok References Home

Open References, choose one Grok account, and repair its remaining online-only references while Chrome is signed in to that same account. The repair keeps working in the background, can be paused or resumed, and clearly separates originals stored locally from links Grok no longer has and items worth retrying.

If your catalog spans several Grok accounts, repeat the repair account by account. GenCatalog waits for the right signed-in account instead of mixing your whole history into one ambiguous run.

Your References, Finally Browsable

References is now a real browser for the source imagery behind your work. Filter by service, account, local-copy state, or when a reference was first used. Sort by most or least used, newest or oldest first use, or most recent use.

Live family and generation counts make the shape of your creative library visible, while every reference clearly identifies whether its preview is safely local, still online, or missing.

Follow Every Branch

Reference details now show the exact local path and can reveal the original in Finder or Explorer. Open every generation derived from a reference, then return with the visible Back action or the Escape key—without resetting the rest of your library.

Every Creative Service, One Menu Away

The desktop Go menu now reaches every supported cloud creation service: Grok Imagine, Midjourney, Higgsfield, Digen, Venice, GPT Image, Gemini, Rogue Studio, and Arcana Labs.

App Polish

Images uploaded to Grok stay in their proper role as references instead of reappearing as promptless generations. Account filters now have a clear, targeted reset, and reference-family views are easier to leave without losing your place.

GenCatalog 5.16.76

Published July 21, 2026

One living catalog for your creative work

This is one of the biggest updates GenCatalog has ever received.

Whether you create in ComfyUI, Forge, InvokeAI, Fooocus, Grok, Higgsfield, Midjourney, or somewhere else, GenCatalog is becoming the place where your creative work lives—preserving not just the files, but how they were made.

Automatic Sources

Connect the output folder you already use with ComfyUI, Forge, InvokeAI, Fooocus, or any other local creative tool.

New images and videos appear in GenCatalog automatically, without creating another copy of your media.

Your originals stay exactly where they are. Pause, rescan, or remove a Source at any time without moving, editing, or deleting your files.

Save the recipe. Not just the file.

Generation Recipes preserve far more than prompts and metadata. Every Recipe captures the creative process itself—from prompts and models to generation stages, resources, workflow evidence, and supported embedded metadata.

Simple creations stay concise. Complex workflows preserve everything.

Each Generation Recipe can include:

  • Source and result imagery
  • A selectable representative video frame
  • Creative intent, prompts, and negative prompts
  • Generation stages
  • Models, LoRAs, samplers, schedulers, and resources
  • Embedded workflow evidence
  • A polished, self-contained PNG document
  • Complete Recipe JSON

Instead of sending screenshots of prompt boxes, you can now share a complete record of how the work was created.

Richer metadata throughout GenCatalog

Generation Recipes aren’t just exportable documents. The same richer metadata now appears throughout GenCatalog itself.

As supported platforms expose more information, detail panels become more complete—making models, workflows, resources, prompts, references, and generation settings easier to browse without leaving your library.

Edit Catalog Entries

Correct or enrich Library entries directly in GenCatalog. You can now edit prompts, negative prompts, model information, and source details while preserving the original media and embedded Recipe evidence.

Your edits remain intact even when GenCatalog revisits the file’s original metadata.

References, clarified

The former Source Images section is now called References. This better distinguishes creative input imagery from the new Sources that automatically bring work into your catalog.

One place for your creative history

Your favorite creative tools don’t need to change.

GenCatalog simply becomes the place where their work comes together—organized locally, searchable instantly, and preserved with the context you’ll still care about months or years from now.

GenCatalog 5.16.21

Published June 16, 2026

Private Vault with Encryption at Rest

Private Vault is now available for generations you want to protect more strongly than the normal library, Hidden Items, App Lock, or Privacy Mode.

When you move an item into your Private Vault, GenCatalog encrypts its media file, thumbnail, prompt, and metadata on disk, then removes the normal plaintext library copies. While the Vault is locked, GenCatalog does not show the Vault contents, counts, names, thumbnails, or prompts.

"Encryption at rest" means the Vault data is stored in encrypted form while it is sitting on your drive. Someone browsing your catalog folder in Finder or File Explorer should not be able to read Vault media or metadata without unlocking the Vault in GenCatalog.

The boundary is important: App Lock keeps the GenCatalog window closed, Privacy Mode hides previews on screen, and Hidden Items keeps items out of the main grid. Private Vault is the feature for encrypted storage. Once you unlock the Vault, its items can be viewed and moved back to the normal library as regular unencrypted files.

Set up your Vault with a Vault password and save the recovery key. On supported Macs, you can also enable Touch ID for day-to-day Vault unlocks.

Capture Fixes for Grok, Venice, and Higgsfield

Grok bulk downloads now avoid treating one previously deleted child generation as a reason to skip other new children from the same parent, so newly created videos can still be found and saved.

Venice saves now refresh the catalog automatically and preserve visible model names such as Seedance when that information appears in the generation view.

Higgsfield saves are safer when you open a reference generation from another Higgsfield detail view, and Nano Banana Pro generations are labeled more consistently.
